# Heather McDonald — independent website redesign concept

An **unofficial, independently prepared** interactive redesign concept by Alison (September 2026). Not commissioned, approved or endorsed by Heather McDonald or her team.

## Explore the designs

If GitHub Pages is enabled for the `main` branch, root folder:

- **Desktop homepage concept:** https://alisoneleece-source.github.io/Heather-McDonald/?desktop=1
- **Curated Shop concept:** https://alisoneleece-source.github.io/Heather-McDonald/shop.html
- **Dedicated mobile homepage:** https://alisoneleece-source.github.io/Heather-McDonald/mobile.html
- **Interactive phone-frame preview for desktop viewers:** https://alisoneleece-source.github.io/Heather-McDonald/preview.html

The homepage automatically opens the separately designed mobile page on narrow screens. Use `?desktop=1` to examine the desktop design on a phone. The preview page contains a scrollable, clickable phone-sized iframe. The Shop page adapts to narrower screens.

## What the concept demonstrates

- A portrait-led desktop homepage in place of the earlier illustrative Juicy Scoop cover, without the peach-colored decoration behind the portrait.
- A **different mobile homepage layout**, with a portrait-first introduction, quick podcast/tour/Shop shortcuts and a persistent Patreon action.
- Direct links to Heather’s Patreon membership selection page. Payments and sign-ups happen on Patreon, **not** in this demo.
- A Shop section on both homepages and a dedicated `shop.html` page grouping **Books, Apparel and Accessories on the concept website itself**, not Shopify collections.
- Merchandise product cards link to verified individual product pages at https://juicyscoopshop.com/. Visitors choose variants and purchase on the actual store, not in this demo.
- Both published books link to their publisher pages. Books are **not represented as listed in Heather’s Shopify store**; adding them there would require the team to confirm product rights, inventory, pricing and fulfillment.
- Variant-specific Shopify cart/checkout URLs are intentionally **not invented**; Heather’s team could substitute them once suitable product and variant information is supplied.
- Podcast and tour routes that link to her official website without invented episode data, event listings, membership benefits or prices.
- Expandable notes explaining design decisions.

## Publication and quality limitations

GitHub accepting files does not establish that GitHub Pages has deployed. To publish: repository **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main → /(root) → Save**. Refresh the Pages settings screen and confirm the live URL. Test desktop, mobile, shop links, image load, navigation and external links before sharing with Heather’s team.

The photo is taken from a publicly displayed Heather McDonald promotional image and is included **only as a provisional visual reference**. Its reuse rights and Heather’s/team’s approval have **not** been confirmed. Replace it with an approved, locally hosted portrait before presenting the concept externally or using it in production. External image hosting can also change or block access; a text fallback is included. Stylized product/book graphics are placeholders rather than official product images.

The demo is not a full production site. Analytics, approved imagery and copy, content feeds, content management integration, comprehensive browser/device/accessibility checks and business requirements would need to be confirmed before implementation.

For Heather and her team

Made for
the phone.

This is a separate mobile-first design, not a shrunken desktop homepage.

01 / Heather takes centre stage

A large portrait leads the experience. The image comes from a publicly displayed promotional image; image rights and approval would need to be confirmed before production use.

02 / Patreon is always within reach

A clear Patreon action appears in the hero, membership section, navigation and persistent bottom bar. Every link opens Heather’s actual membership selection page.

03 / Less navigation, clearer choices

The phone layout prioritizes two shortcuts: listen to the show or find a live performance. Supporting content follows naturally.

04 / Current information stays current

The demo links to official episode, tour and membership destinations instead of making up live data or copying prices that may change.

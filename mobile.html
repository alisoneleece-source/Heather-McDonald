Made for
the phone.

This is a separately designed, mobile-first experience, not a shrunken desktop homepage.

01 / Heather takes centre stage

A large portrait leads the experience. Image rights and approval must be confirmed before production or external presentation.

02 / Patreon is always within reach

The hero, navigation and membership section provide Patreon links. A persistent bottom action remains visible while scrolling. Links open Heather’s real Patreon membership page.

03 / Clearer mobile choices

Quick shortcuts lead to the podcast and live shows, with less navigation to scan.

04 / Keep information current

This concept links to official episode, show and membership information rather than copying prices or inventing schedules.

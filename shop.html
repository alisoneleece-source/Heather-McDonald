For Heather and her team

One look. One place. Clear next steps.

The categories above are website navigation, not Shopify collections. This is a proposed front-end organization for product links, not a change to Heather’s Shopify account.

No orders are processed on this demonstration.

01 · Organize on the website

Books, Apparel and Accessories each have their own browseable section, regardless of how items appear in Shopify.

02 · Link individual products

Cards currently lead to verified official product pages where visitors can choose a size or colour. Heather’s team could substitute variant-specific cart or checkout links where appropriate.

03 · Add books when arrangements are confirmed

Books currently link to the publisher. Direct Shopify purchasing would require the team to list them and confirm inventory, pricing and fulfillment.

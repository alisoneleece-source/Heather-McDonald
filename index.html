For Heather and her team

Why these
changes?

This independent, clickable concept demonstrates clearer routes to listening, joining, shopping and seeing Heather live. It is not an approved design or a replacement for her live site.

Production would require image permission, content approval and input from the team.

Try the dedicated phone preview ↗
Heather’s portrait replaces a decorative mock podcast cover. It brings the person and personality behind the show into the first impression. The photo is an illustrative placeholder until image rights and brand approval are confirmed.
Membership links appear in navigation, the hero, the podcast card and a focused membership section. All go to the actual Patreon membership selection page rather than a simulated checkout.
A separately designed mobile page uses a portrait-first hero, quick listening and tour shortcuts, a prominent membership section and a persistent Patreon action. Open the mobile preview link to try it inside a phone frame on desktop.
The concept links to official show, tour, merchandise and Patreon destinations rather than inventing episodes, venues or prices. Production could use approved integrations to display current information automatically.
The Shop page groups books, apparel and accessories on the website itself. Merchandise cards link to verified product pages, while books link to publisher pages until Heather’s team can offer them in Shopify. Variant-specific cart or checkout links can be supplied by the team later; the demo does not imitate checkout.

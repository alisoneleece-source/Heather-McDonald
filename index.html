For Heather and her team

Why these
changes?

This independent, clickable concept demonstrates a clearer visitor journey. It is not a replacement for Heather’s live website or an approved brand design.

Production copy, imagery, publishing integrations and analytics would need the team’s input.

Compare with the current website ↗
Patreon appears in the navigation, hero, podcast section and a dedicated membership section. Every membership button goes directly to Heather’s actual Patreon membership page, not a pretend checkout.
Podcast discovery, Patreon memberships and live shows have identifiable destinations. Supporting biographical information remains easy to reach without competing for attention.
The layout adapts to smaller screens, navigation opens with an accessible button and primary links are touch-friendly. Production would still require a complete accessibility audit.
The proposal directs visitors to official tour and membership pages rather than copying dates, benefits or prices that can become outdated. A production site could use approved integrations.

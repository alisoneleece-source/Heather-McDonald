Why these
changes?

This independent, clickable concept demonstrates clearer routes to listening, watching, joining, shopping, meeting Heather, contacting representation and seeing her live. It is not an approved design or a replacement for her live site.

Production requires image permission, content approval and input from the team.

Try the dedicated phone preview ↗
Heather’s portrait brings her personality into the first impression. Photo approval remains unconfirmed.
A dedicated Juicy Scoop page connects listeners to the current Apple Podcasts listing, the YouTube channel and Patreon instead of relying on outdated podcast links.
The separately designed mobile homepage has a portrait-first hero, quick links and a persistent Patreon action.
The Scoop on Heather presents her supplied biography as a portrait-led editorial profile instead of a short list of credits.
The Shop visually groups books, apparel and accessories on this website. Merchandise links lead to the official shop, and books link to publisher pages until the team adds them to Shopify.

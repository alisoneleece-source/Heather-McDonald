01 / The podcast

There’s always more to scoop.

Heather McDonald hosts Juicy Scoop with Heather McDonald, the pop culture podcast where she and celebrity guests share the inside scoop. Her loyal Juicy Scoopers keep coming back for the laughs and conversations.

The biography provided for this concept describes the show as being in its ninth year, with more than 1,000 episodes, 200 million downloads and 22,482 five-star iTunes reviews. It also describes the show as the number-one female comedy interview podcast and cites a number-two placement on the Comedy Interview chart.

02 / On stage

The jokes don’t stop at the mic.

Heather’s stand-up specials have been released on Showtime, Amazon Prime and Netflix. Her more recent special, Juicy Scoop, received enthusiastic five-star audience reviews in its first two weeks, according to her biography. Her debut special, I Don’t Mean to Brag, was released on Showtime and Netflix.

She is a headlining comedian who has sold-out shows at theaters and clubs. Her stand-up comedy, podcast and other creative work are known for their relatability.

03 / The personal side

Life, laughter and girls’ night out.

Heather has shared stories about her marriage of more than 24 years, her two teenage sons and life as a stepmother, all while keeping girls’ nights out a priority in her storytelling.

Her celebrity impressions and comedic pop culture sketches have gone viral across social platforms and continue to entertain audiences.

04 / On screen and behind the scenes

From the writers’ room to the spotlight.

Heather is recognized for E!’s Chelsea Lately, which she wrote, produced and appeared on throughout its seven-year run. She also starred in the mockumentary series After Lately.

She was a contributing writer and had featured roles in the Wayans brothers’ films White Chicks and Dance Flicks, among others.

Heather has hosted on TLC and appeared on Bravo’s Watch What Happens Live, The Bachelorette, Wendy Williams, HLN, Nightline, CNN, 25 Words or Less, Punchline, RuPaul’s Drag Race, The Tonight Show and Keeping Up With the Kardashians, among other programs.

05 / In print

Two books. More stories.

Heather has written two New York Times bestselling books: You’ll Never Blue Ball in This Town Again and My Inappropriate Life.
